# Surface brief: the board

Mode: Operate. Audience: a job seeker who is also an engineer, checking in on an agent that acts in their name. Task: see what was sent, answer what is blocking, add a job, take cards over.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One screen, the board. The agent works without being asked, and its hands are visible on the cards while it works. Refuses the chat-app arrangement: the conversation exists only for the agent's questions.

OWN-WORLD: Dproz. The wordmark in logo blue, Dproz blue for the one primary action and for "the agent is acting", the Dproz grey ramp for ground and ink, and a pastel header per column in that column's hue (blue found, yellow waiting on you, indigo ready, violet yours to send, green applied, teal interview, grey closed). White cards with a hairline edge on a grey column tray, inside a white panel inset on a grey canvas. Plus Jakarta Sans throughout. Follows the system light or dark setting.

STORY: I gave it my resume. I can see what it sent and exactly what was sent, I can see what is waiting on me, and I trust it enough to leave it running.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Icon rail at left. The panel carries the wordmark, the title, the agent's status with pause, and Add a job. Below, columns with coloured headers fill the width. The questions panel opens at right only when the agent asks or the user wants the log.

FORM: Kanban, pinned by the brief (the user asked for a Kanban board and supplied a reference). No concept roll was dealt.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Every card a subagent is working on shows its current step live, several at once, and cards glide between columns when the agent moves them.
